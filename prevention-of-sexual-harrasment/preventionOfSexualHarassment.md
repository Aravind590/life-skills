#Prevention Of Sexual Harassment

---------
## **Questions**


**1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?**

- comments on the clothing and body 
- sexual or gender based jokes
- inappropirate touchings
- sexual gesturing , leering or staring
- visual harrasment such as obscence posters and screensavers , etc



**2.  What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?**


If I face sexual harassment, I will speak up if it is safe, take the evidence, and report it to my HR or to the Internal Committee. If anyone  witness it, I will support the affected person and report what I saw.
